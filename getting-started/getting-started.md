# Getting started

Welcome to the getting started guide for Owlog. This section will help you create an account, sign in, and begin exploring the platform. Happy Blogging :)

By the end of this guide, you will be able to:

* Create and verify your Owlog account.
* Understand that email verification is required before signing in and that you can request a new verification email from the login page.
* Sign in to the platform using the "Remember Me" option if desired.
* Ensure your password meets the minimum requirement of at least 8 characters.
* Navigate the homepage and discover content.

## Pages in this section

* **Creating an account**: Learn how to create your Owlog account, verify your email address, request another verification email if needed, and reset your password if you forget it.
* **Signing in**: A step-by-step guide to signing in to your account, including the email verification requirement and the Remember Me option.
* **Exploring the homepage**: An introduction to the layout of the Owlog homepage and how to find your way around.
