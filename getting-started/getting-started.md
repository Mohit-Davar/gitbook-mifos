# Getting started

Welcome to the getting started guide for Owlog. This section will help you create an account, sign in, and begin exploring the platform. Happy Blogging :)

By the end of this guide, you will be able to:

* Create your Owlog account and verify your email (email verification is required before signing in).
* Sign in to the platform (including using the "Remember Me" option and meeting the password minimum of 8 characters).
* Request a new verification email from the sign-in page if needed.
* Navigate the homepage and discover content.

## Pages in this section

* **Creating an account**: Learn how to create your Owlog account, verify your email address, and reset your password if you forget it.
* **Signing in**: A step-by-step guide to signing in to your account.
* **Exploring the homepage**: An introduction to the layout of the Owlog homepage and how to find your way around.
